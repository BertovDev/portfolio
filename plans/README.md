## Findings considered and rejected / deferred (so nobody re-audits them)

- **`BakeShadows` freezing shadow maps while physics bodies fall** — currently harmless (diplomas don't `castShadow`). Documented in plan 004's maintenance notes; no action.
- **`colliders="hull"` on the 4.8 K-tri `AboutModel`** — one-time QuickHull at load, probably < 20 ms; unmeasured. Deferred; noted in plan 004.
- **Portfolio hover re-renders the whole model and mounts 8 `<Outlines>`** — real but a MED-confidence refactor of a 340-line gltfjsx file; visible only as a first-hover hitch. Deferred until 004–006 land and it can be measured in isolation.
- **Gate `TiltShift2`/`Bloom` off on low-end devices** — art-direction call for the owner; plan 004 only makes them cheaper.
- **KTX2/Basis GPU texture compression** — larger VRAM win than WebP but needs `KTX2Loader` + transcoder wiring and `gl.detectSupport`. WebP (plan 007) captures most of the download win with zero loader changes. Revisit if VRAM on mobile becomes the bottleneck.
- **`<link rel="preload">` for `portfolio.glb`** — `GLTFLoader` fetches with its own request mode; a mismatched preload double-downloads. Not worth the risk without browser verification.
- **`/api/send` payload bounds / rate limit** — legitimate but a security/correctness item, not perf; out of this focus.
- **`model.d.ts` has three `export default` statements** (only the last survives, so `Portfolio.tsx`/`AboutModel.tsx` are typed against the mail GLB) — correctness, not perf. Worth a separate fix.
- **Hosting videos off-repo (Blob/CDN)** — sensible follow-up after plan 007 shrinks them.
- **`Contact.tsx` canvas** — already well configured (`frameloop="demand"`, `performance.min`, `AdaptiveDpr`, `shadows={false}`). Clean.
- **`layout.tsx`, fonts loading strategy, `<img>` usage, `globals.css`, GSAP barrel imports, scroll/resize listeners, `setState` in `useFrame`, per-frame allocations in `useFrame`, rapier `debug`, trimesh colliders** — audited, clean.
